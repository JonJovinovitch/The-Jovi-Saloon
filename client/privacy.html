Privacy Policy

The Jovi Saloon · last updated 2026-08-14

The Jovi Saloon is a poker room for a private group of friends, run as a Discord Activity and as a plain web app. This page explains what information it touches and why.

What we collect

  • Your Discord identity. When you open the activity inside Discord, we ask Discord for your username, display name, and avatar so the table can show who you are. We do not receive your email, friends list, messages, or anything else from your Discord account.
  • The name you type. If you play in a browser outside Discord, we ask for a display name instead. It is stored only in your own browser (localStorage), not on our server.
  • Gameplay state. Seat, stack, cards, chat messages and the character you picked, while a game is running.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no passwords, no real money. Chips are play money with no cash value.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers.
  • No cookies. The only thing saved in your browser is your display name, your chosen character, and a sound on/off preference — all in localStorage, readable only by this app, on your own device.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone. There is nobody to sell it to — this is a hobby project for one friend group.

How long we keep it

Room and game state lives in server memory only. It is discarded automatically a couple of minutes after everyone disconnects, and it is wiped entirely whenever the server restarts or is redeployed. Nothing is written to a database or a disk.

Third parties

The Discord OAuth exchange happens directly with Discord, per Discord's own Privacy Policy. This app does not send your data to any other third party.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is only usable by people already in the Discord server it is installed to.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
